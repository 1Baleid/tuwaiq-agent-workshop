# Transcript Information (the key printed with KFUPM transcripts, with current rules)

## About this document

This is the information printed with KFUPM transcripts. It is an older official document; where the KFUPM Registrar has since changed a rule (graduation honors and semester honors), the current rule is given below (notice added on 30 October 2026).

## Grading system since September 1997 (quality points per semester hour)

Effective First Semester 1997-1998 KFUPM applied the new grading system. Academic calendar: two semesters of 15 weeks and a summer session of 8 weeks. Grades and quality points per semester hour:

- A+ Exceptional 4.00; A Excellent 3.75; B+ Superior 3.50; B Very Good 3.00; C+ Above Average 2.50; C Good 2.00; D+ High Pass 1.50; D Pass 1.00; F Fail 0.00.
- IP In Progress (no points); IC Incomplete (no points); DN Denial 0.00; NP No grade-pass (no points); NF No grade-fail (no points); W Withdrawn (no points); WP Withdrawn with Pass (no points); WF Withdrawn with Fail 0.00; AU Audit (no points).

## Grading system before September 1997 (old transcripts)

Grades on transcripts from before September 1997, with quality points per semester hour: A Excellent 4; B Good 3; C Fair 2; D Passing 1; F Failing 0; WF Withdrawn while failing 0. Grades without points: P Pass; W Withdrawn; WP Withdrawn with pass; I Incomplete; IP In-progress; Z temporary grade to be changed to WP or WF; AU Audit.

## Repeating courses, academic standing and graduation requirements (transcript key)

Repeat of course work: a student may repeat 'D+ or below' grades only, and all the grades will appear on the student's transcript.

Academic standing: a student is considered in good academic standing unless otherwise indicated.

Graduation requirements for undergraduate students: (1) the student has to complete all courses required by his major department including the courses specified in his curriculum; (2) a cumulative GPA and major GPA of 2.00 or better.

## Semester honors on transcripts (old criteria) and the current rule

Old semester honors (named First/Second/Third Honors before September 1997, First/Second/Third Distinction from September 1997): first: semester GPA 3.75 - 4.00 and 60 or more semester quality points; second: semester GPA 3.50 - 3.74 and 56 or more; third: semester GPA 3.00 - 3.49 and 48 or more.

[UPDATED RULE] New criteria: First Distinction: semester GPA > 3.85 and quality points > 58; Second Distinction: semester GPA > 3.70 and quality points > 56; Third Distinction: semester GPA > 3.50 and quality points > 53. Applied in phases: Preparatory students from Term 241, Freshmen from Term 251, Sophomores from Term 261, Juniors from Term 271, Seniors from Term 281. Until their phase starts, Juniors and Seniors keep the old criteria.

## Graduation honors on transcripts (old criteria) and the current rule

Before September 1997: Highest Honors 3.75 - 4.00; High Honors 3.50 - 3.74; Honors 3.00 - 3.49 (cumulative GPA). From September 1997: First Honors 3.750 - 4.000; Second Honors 3.250 - 3.749; Third Honors 3.000 - 3.249. Effective September 1997, in order to be eligible for the first or the second honors the student must not have failed in any course at the university he is currently attending or any other university.

[UPDATED RULE] The First, Second and Third Honors criteria continue until the end of Term 281 (First Semester 2028-2029). Effective Term 282 (Second Semester 2028-2029): Highest Honors: cumulative GPA > 3.85; High Honors: cumulative GPA > 3.70; Honors: cumulative GPA > 3.50.
