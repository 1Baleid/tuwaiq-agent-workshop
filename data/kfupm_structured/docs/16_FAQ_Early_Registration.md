# FAQ: Early Registration

## When can I early register for the following term?

See the early registration events for the following term provided on the Office of the University Registrar (OUR) website.

## How is undergraduate early registration organized?

The early registration for undergraduate students is categorized based on the students' class level. Steps to check your class level: https://registrar.kfupm.edu.sa/docs/pdf/Steps_to_check_Class_Level.pdf. The unified class level rule table: https://registrar.kfupm.edu.sa/docs/pdf/Unified_Undergraduate_Class_Level_Rule_Table.pdf

## Must a preparatory student about to become a freshman early register?

Yes, preparatory students who are expected to be promoted to freshmen at the end of the current term should register at least 13 credit hours during the early registration period for the following term (see the early registration events for designated timings).

## How do I add, drop or change a section during early registration?

You can do these activities through the portal during the designated period (see the early registration events).

## Do I have to register at least 13 credit hours during early registration?

Yes, you must register at least 13 credit hours during the early registration period. Students who are expected to graduate in the following term and are left with less than 13 credit hours to complete graduation requirements are exempted from this rule.

## How do I add a closed section during early registration?

You can submit a closed section approval through the e-petitions link in the portal. This also applies if you are currently registered in less than 13 credit hours and the course you intend to register is closed.

## I have an Advisor Approval hold for early registration. What should I do?

You need to contact your advisor for his approval for early registration and to release the hold.

## Do I register Co-op, Internship or Summer Training again during early registration?

No, students who have already registered for a Co-op, Internship or Summer Training program in the following term are NOT required to register it again during the early registration period.

## How do I drop Co-op, Internship or Summer Training and early register for courses?

If you have already registered for a Co-op, Internship or Summer Training program in the following term, you need to contact the Training department at the Deanship of Student Affairs in this regard.

## Is there a limit on transactions, and what if I forget my Portal password?

Yes, each student is assigned a limited number of transactions.

In case you forgot your password, contact the IT help desk. Students are advised to visit the portal and ensure that they can log in without any problems before the early registration period.
