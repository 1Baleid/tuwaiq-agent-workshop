# Replacement of a Lost or Damaged Graduation Certificate (Office of the University Registrar)

## Conditions for a replacement graduation certificate (penalty 2000 SAR)

A replacement for a lost or damaged graduation certificate will be issued as per the following conditions:

- The graduate should bring his non-expired national ID, given that the name on the new certificate will be identical to that on the national ID.
- A letter from the employer showing that they do not have the original certificate, in case the graduate is an employee.
- Sign the pledge to take responsibility for losing the certificate.
- Pay a penalty of 2000 SAR.

## Pledge for replacement of a lost certificate

The graduate fills in his name and student ID, signs and dates this pledge: "I request a replacement for a lost certificate, and I pledge that I do not possess the two certificates simultaneously. Accordingly, the university administration and the responsible authorities in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia have the right to sue me if otherwise was proven. In addition, I pledge that as soon as I find the lost certificate, I will inform the university immediately and hand it over, or I will be responsible for the consequences."

## Pledge for replacement of a damaged certificate

The graduate fills in his name and student ID, signs and dates this pledge: "I request a replacement for a damaged certificate, and I pledge that I do not possess the two certificates simultaneously. Accordingly, the university administration and the responsible authorities in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia have the right to sue me if otherwise was proven. In addition, I pledge that I did not intentionally damage the certificate and that I have handed it over to the university."
