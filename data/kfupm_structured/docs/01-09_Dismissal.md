# Undergraduate Regulations, Section 9: Dismissal (updated with current rules)

## Dismissal for not finishing the Freshman level in three regular terms (updated rule)

[UPDATED RULE] New academic regulation: undergraduate students are limited to a maximum of three regular terms to complete the Freshman level, including any dropped, withdrawn, or no-show term. Students who do not complete the Freshman level within three regular terms (including dropped, withdrawn, or no-show terms) are subject to dismissal at the end of the third regular term. Source: KFUPM Registrar announcement "New Academic Regulation: Restriction limiting undergraduate students to a maximum of three regular terms to complete the Freshman level".

## Dismissal after three consecutive academic probations (Article Twenty, rules 1 and 2)

1. A student is dismissed if he receives three consecutive academic probations.
2. Following the recommendation of the Deanship of Admissions & Registration in coordination with the college council, the University Council may allow the student a fourth opportunity to improve his cumulative GPA.

## Dismissal for taking too long to graduate (Article Twenty, rule 3)

A student is dismissed if he fails to complete the graduation requirements within an additional period equal to one half of the original program's duration. The University Council, based upon the recommendation of the Deanship of Admissions & Registration in coordination with the college council, may exempt the student from this restriction and give him the opportunity to complete the graduation requirements within an additional period of maximum duration equal to that of the original program.

## Final extension of at most two regular semesters (Article Twenty, rule 4)

A student is dismissed if he fails to complete the graduation requirements within an additional period equal to that of the original program's duration. Following the recommendation of the Deanship of Admissions & Registration in coordination with the college council, the University Council may grant the student an additional opportunity not exceeding two regular semesters to complete the graduation requirements.

## After dismissal: notification and clearance form (Article Twenty, rules 5 and 6)

The Deanship of Admissions & Registration informs the student of his dismissal and cancels his enrollment.

A dismissed student is obliged to obtain a clearance form from the Deanship of Student Affairs and have it signed by all the relevant departments as mentioned in Article Nineteen.
