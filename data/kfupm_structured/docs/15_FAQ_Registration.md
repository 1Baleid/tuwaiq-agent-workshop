# FAQ: Registration

## Do I need to confirm my registration if I early-registered?

Yes, all Preparatory, undergraduate and graduate students (except those who are registered in Co-op/summer training) should submit registration confirmation through the KFUPM Portal during the registration confirmation period.

## What happens if I do not confirm my registration?

All your courses will be dropped after the deadline for registration confirmation.

## Do Co-op, Internship or Summer Training students need to confirm registration?

No, students who are expected to go on a Co-op, Internship or Summer Training program are not required to submit registration confirmation through the portal.

## How do I remove a registration hold?

Students who have a registration hold should contact the concerned office for hold clearance. Upon getting clearance, they need to contact the Registrar's office to remove the hold.

## How do I add, drop or change a section?

You can do these activities through the portal during the designated period.

## Do I have to register at least 13 credit hours this term?

Yes, as per academic regulations, undergraduate students must register at least 13 credit hours in the current term. Students who are expected to graduate in the current term and are left with less than 13 credit hours to complete graduation requirements are exempted from this rule.

## I registered a course whose prerequisite I have not passed. What should I do?

If you early-registered in course(s) which require a prerequisite that has not been taken (or was failed in previous terms), you need to drop the course which requires the prerequisite and add some other course during the registration period.

## How do I add a closed section?

Undergraduate students can submit an e-petition to the department for closed section approval.

## I am a newly promoted freshman and have not registered before. How do I register?

Students who are newly promoted to the freshmen level will be able to add courses through the portal during the designated period (see the registration events).

## How do I drop Co-op, Internship or Summer Training and register for courses instead?

If you have already registered for a Co-op, Internship or Summer Training program in the current term, you need to contact the Training department at the Deanship of Student Affairs in this regard.

## Is there a limit on the number of registration transactions?

Yes, each student is assigned a limited number of transactions.

## What should I do if I forget my Portal password?

In case you forgot your password, contact the IT help desk. In addition, students are advised to visit the portal and ensure that they can log in without any problems before the registration period.
