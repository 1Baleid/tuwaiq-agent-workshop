# Undergraduate Regulations, Section 16: Appendices

## Appendix A: the academic record

The academic record is a statement which explains the student's academic progress. It includes the courses studied in each term with course numbers, codes, number of credit-hours, the grades attained and the codes and points of these grades. The record also shows the semester, cumulative GPA and the student's academic status in addition to the courses from which a transferred student is waived.

## Appendix A and C: letter grades with marks and points

Each letter grade with its marks, points out of 4.00 and points out of 5.00:

- A+: 95-100, 4.00, 5.00, Exceptional
- A: 90 - less than 95, 3.75, 4.75, Excellent
- B+: 85 - less than 90, 3.50, 4.50, Superior
- B: 80 - less than 85, 3.00, 4.00, Very Good
- C+: 75 - less than 80, 2.50, 3.50, Above Average
- C: 70 - less than 75, 2.00, 3.00, Good
- D+: 65 - less than 70, 1.50, 2.50, High-Pass
- D: 60 - less than 65, 1.00, 2.00, Pass
- F: less than 60, 0.00, 1.00, Fail

## Appendix A and C: other grade codes (IP, IC, DN, NP, NF, W, WP, WF, AU)

- IP: In-Progress (no points)
- IC: Incomplete (no points)
- DN: Denial, 0.00 points out of 4.00 (1.00 out of 5.00)
- NP: No grade-Pass, marks 60 or above (no points)
- NF: No grade-Fail, marks less than 60 (no points)
- W: Withdrawn (no points)
- WP: Withdrawn with Pass (no points)
- WF: Withdrawn with Fail (no points)
- AU: Audit (no points)

## Appendix B: example GPA calculation, first semester

Quality points = credit hours x grade points. Example first semester (course, credit hours, marks, grade, quality points out of 5.00, quality points out of 4.00):

- IAS 301: 2 credit hours, 85, B+, 9, 7
- CHEM 324: 3 credit hours, 70, C, 9, 6
- MATH 235: 3 credit hours, 92, A, 14.25, 11.25
- PHYS 312: 4 credit hours, 80, B, 16, 12
- Total: 12 credit hours, 48.25 and 36.25 quality points

Semester GPA = quality points / credit hours (calculated from the totals): 36.25 / 12 = 3.02 out of 4.00 (48.25 / 12 = 4.02 out of 5.00).

## Appendix B: example GPA calculation, second semester and cumulative GPA

Example second semester (course, credit hours, marks, grade, quality points out of 5.00, quality points out of 4.00):

- IAS 104: 2 credit hours, 96, A+, 10, 8
- CHEM 327: 3 credit hours, 83, B, 12, 9
- MATH 314: 4 credit hours, 71, C, 12, 8
- PHYS 326: 3 credit hours, 81, B, 12, 9
- Total: 12 credit hours, 46 and 34 quality points

Calculated from the totals: semester GPA 34 / 12 = 2.83 out of 4.00. Cumulative GPA after both semesters = (36.25 + 34) / (12 + 12) = 2.93 out of 4.00.
