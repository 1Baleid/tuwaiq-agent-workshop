# Academic Calendar, Next Semester: Second Semester 2026-2027 (Term 262, 1448H-1449H)

## Term 262: start of classes, adding and dropping courses

- Sunday Jan. 10, 2027 (week 1, 2 Sha'ban 1448): registration confirmation through the KFUPM Portal; classes begin.
- Tuesday Jan. 12, 2027 (week 1): last day for registration confirmation (until 11:59 PM); last day for adding courses.
- Thursday Jan. 21, 2027 (week 2): last day for dropping course(s) without permanent record.

## Term 262: advising and Co-op or Summer Training registration

- Jan. 24 - Feb. 4, 2027 (weeks 3-4): SEP updates and approval by the academic advisor (2 weeks).
- Jan. 31 - Feb. 4, 2027 (week 4): registration for Co-op/Internship in 263/271 and Summer Training 263.

## Term 262: Founding Day and Eid Al-Fitr holidays

- Sunday - Monday Feb. 21 - 22, 2027 (week 7): Saudi Founding Day holiday.
- Sunday Feb. 28 - Thursday March 11, 2027: Eid Al-Fitr holidays.
- Sunday March 14, 2027 (week 8): classes resume after the Ramadan break.

## Term 262: midterm grades and withdrawal deadlines (W grade)

- March 14 - March 25, 2027 (weeks 8-9): midterm grade reports through the KFUPM Portal (2 weeks).
- Thursday April 1, 2027 (week 10): last day for dropping course(s) with a grade of "W" through the KFUPM Portal.
- Thursday April 29, 2027 (week 14): last day for withdrawal from all courses with a grade of "W" (approval from Student Affairs is required through irshad.kfupm.edu.sa). This is also the last day for major exams.

## Term 262: early registration for term 271 and Eid Al-Adha

- Saturday April 17 - Sunday April 25, 2027 (weeks 12-14): early registration for the First Semester 2027-2028 (271).
- Sunday May 9 - Thursday May 20, 2027: Eid Al-Adha holidays.
- Sunday May 23, 2027 (week 16): classes resume after the Eid Al-Adha holidays.

## Term 262: last day of classes, final examinations and graduation date

- Monday May 24, 2027 (week 16): last day of classes.
- Tuesday May 25 - Sunday June 6, 2027 (weeks 16-18): final examinations.
- Tuesday June 8, 2027 (week 18): last day for faculty to submit grades to the Deanship (2:00 PM); official graduation date.
- Sunday June 20, 2027: beginning of Co-op and Summer Training, and registration confirmation for Summer Semester 263.
