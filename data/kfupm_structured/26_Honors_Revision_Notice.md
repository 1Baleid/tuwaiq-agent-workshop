# Revision of Academic Regulations for Undergraduate Graduation Honors and Semester Honors (Registrar notice)

## New graduation honors: Highest Honors above 3.85, High Honors above 3.70, Honors above 3.50

Based on the university council approval, the criteria for undergraduate graduation honors have been revised:

- Highest Honors: cumulative GPA > 3.85
- High Honors: cumulative GPA > 3.70
- Honors: cumulative GPA > 3.50

## When the new graduation honors start: Term 282

The new graduation honors criteria will be implemented effective Second Semester 2028-2029 (Term 282). Awarding of graduation honors is subject to academic rules and regulations. The current criteria for First Honors, Second Honors and Third Honors will continue until the end of First Semester 2028-2029 (Term 281).

## New semester honors (distinction) criteria

Based on the university council approval, the criteria for semester honors have been revised:

- First Distinction: semester GPA > 3.85 and quality points > 58
- Second Distinction: semester GPA > 3.70 and quality points > 56
- Third Distinction: semester GPA > 3.50 and quality points > 53

Awarding of semester honors is subject to academic rules and regulations.

## When the new semester honors apply to each class level

The new semester honors criteria are implemented in a phased manner:

- First Semester 2024-2025 (Term 241) onwards: Preparatory students.
- First Semester 2025-2026 (Term 251) onwards: Freshmen students (in addition to Preparatory students).
- First Semester 2026-2027 (Term 261) onwards: Sophomore students (in addition to Preparatory and Freshmen students).
- First Semester 2027-2028 (Term 271) onwards: Junior students (in addition to Preparatory, Freshmen and Sophomore students).
- First Semester 2028-2029 (Term 281) onwards: Senior students (in addition to Preparatory, Freshmen, Sophomore and Junior students).
