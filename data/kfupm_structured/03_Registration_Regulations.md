# Registration Regulations (actions that need approval)

## Adding or changing to a closed section

Adding or changing to a closed section is not allowed without approval from the Chairman of the department offering the course.

## Taking a course without its prerequisite or co-requisite

Adding a course without its prerequisite or co-requisite is not allowed without an e-petition approved by the student's Department Chairman, the Chairman of the department offering the course, and the College Dean of the course.

## Overload in good standing: 21 credit hours

Exceeding the maximum credits (overload) needs approval:

- A good standing graduating student with CGPA > 2 is allowed to register 21 credit hours in a regular term and 9 credit hours in a summer term, with an e-petition approved by the Department Chairman.
- A good standing student with CGPA > 3 is allowed to register 21 credit hours in a regular term, with an e-petition approved by the Department Chairman.
- A good standing student with CGPA > 2 is allowed to register 21 credit hours in the regular term preceding his summer graduation term, with an e-petition approved by the Department Chairman.

## Overload on Academic Warning or Probation: 16 to 19 credit hours

- A student on Academic Warning or Probation is allowed to register between 16 and 17 credit hours with an e-petition approved by the Department Chairman.
- A student on Academic Warning or Probation is allowed to register between 18 and 19 credit hours with an e-petition approved by the Department Chairman and the College Dean.
