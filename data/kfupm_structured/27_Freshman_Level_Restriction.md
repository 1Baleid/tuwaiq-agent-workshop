# New Academic Regulation: Maximum of Three Regular Terms to Complete the Freshman Level

## The rule: at most three regular terms at the Freshman level

Undergraduate students shall be limited to a maximum of three regular terms to complete the Freshman level, including any dropped, withdrawn, or no-show term. Limiting completion to three terms will ensure timely progression, promote academic continuity, and support graduation within the prescribed timeframe.

## The consequence: dismissal at the end of the third regular term

Undergraduate students who do not complete the Freshmen level within three regular terms, including dropped, withdrawn, or no-show terms, shall be subject to dismissal at the end of the third regular term.
