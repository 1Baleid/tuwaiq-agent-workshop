# FAQ: Course Offerings in the Summer Term

## Is the summer term mandatory for the university or the student?

No. It is optional for the university to offer a summer term, and it is optional for the student to register in a summer term.

## Should I wait for the summer term to take a course?

No. As the early registration period is approaching, all college level students are advised to plan their course registration according to the regular semesters' course offering, i.e. not to delay taking certain courses until the summer term. Summer course offerings are highly subjective and are not guaranteed, since the summer term is optional for both the university and the student.
