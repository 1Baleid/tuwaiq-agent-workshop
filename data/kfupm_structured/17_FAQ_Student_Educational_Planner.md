# FAQ: Student Educational Planner (SEP)

## Is there a guide or tutorial for building a plan in SEP?

Yes, guidelines as well as a tutorial for SEP for students can be viewed on the Office of the University Registrar (OUR) website.

## I built my plan in SEP. What next? (advisor locks the plan)

Ensure that your plan is active, and then contact your advisor to lock your plan. Locking of the plan by the advisor will be considered as an approval for early registration.

## I built my plan last term. Does my advisor need to lock it again?

Yes, students who have already built their plan in a previous term also need to contact their advisors to lock their plan, as all the plans locked in the previous term by the advisors have been unlocked for students to make modifications (if any).

## Do I need to plan every term until graduation?

Yes, you need to build the plan for all terms until graduation. It will help you to track your progress as well as help the academic departments to predict course demand in future terms.

## How can I find out who my academic advisor is?

Go to the KFUPM portal and click on the Registrar Services tab. In the Academic Profile channel, select the current term and you will be able to view your academic advisor's name.

## Can I change my SEP plan after the advisor approved it?

Yes, you need to contact your advisor to unlock your plan in order to make modifications. Then, you need to ask your advisor to lock your plan again.

## Do Co-op, Internship or Summer Training students need an SEP plan?

Yes, all undergraduate students must prepare an academic plan for all upcoming terms until graduation, including those currently registered or early registered in Internship, Co-op or Summer Training.

## Can I early register without my advisor's approval?

No, in order to register in the following term(s) you must get your advisor's approval for early registration.

## Do Senior students need to build a plan?

All undergraduate students must prepare a plan for all terms until graduation. Only students who are expected to graduate in the current term don't need to build a plan.

## Can I register a course that is not in my plan?

Yes. In case you couldn't register one of the courses in your plan, you can register another course based on the situation and then make the changes in your plan later. It is advisable to follow your locked plan, but you can register other courses based on course availability and later adjust your plan accordingly.

## How do I remove an SEP hold?

In order to remove the hold, you must contact your advisor to lock your active plan and then remove the hold.
