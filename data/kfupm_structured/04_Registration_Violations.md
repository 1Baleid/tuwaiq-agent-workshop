# Registration Violations and Their Consequences (with current rules)

## About this list

This is the KFUPM list of registration violations and what happens for each. Two violations (#5 and #6) have since been changed by the KFUPM Registrar; the current rules are given below (notice added on 30 October 2026).

## Violation 1: not confirming registration during registration week

Students who do not confirm their registration during the registration week: all courses will be dropped after the last day for registration confirmation. Contact the Registrar in person or via email to confirm your registration.

## Violation 2: lecture without its lab, or lab without its lecture

Student registered in a lecture without its lab or vice versa: the course will be dropped if both lecture and lab are not registered.

## Violation 3: courses together with Co-op, Internship or Summer Training

Students registered in courses in addition to Co-op, Internship or Summer Training programs: courses other than the Co-op, Internship or Summer Training will be dropped. Students should keep either Co-op, Internship or Summer Training, or courses, as per the academic regulations.

## Violation 4: repeating a course already passed with C or better, or with IC

Undergraduate students repeating a course in which they have previously obtained either a 'C' or better grade or an 'IC' grade: the course will be dropped. A student should not register a course in which he had obtained either a 'C' or better grade or an 'IC' grade.

## Violation 5: not in good standing and over 15 credit hours (current rule)

Undergraduate students NOT in good standing exceeding 15 credit hours in a regular semester or 7 credit hours in a summer semester: higher number courses will be dropped until the maximum load is reached (15 credit hours in a regular semester and 7 credit hours in a summer semester).

[UPDATED RULE] A student on Academic Warning or Probation may register 16 to 17 credit hours with an e-petition approved by the Department Chairman, or 18 to 19 credit hours with an e-petition approved by the Department Chairman and the College Dean. Without this approval, the 15-credit-hour maximum still applies.

## Violation 6: registered in fewer than 13 credit hours (current rule)

[UPDATED RULE] Effective term 242, the minimum course load for undergraduate students in a regular term is 13 credit hours (previously 12). Undergraduate students registered in less than 13 credit hours in a regular semester will have all courses dropped after the last day for adding courses. Students expected to graduate in that term and left with fewer than 13 credit hours to complete graduation requirements are excluded from this rule.

## Violations 7 and 8: course restrictions and co-requisites

Violation 7: a student registers a course having a specific restriction (class level, major, college, etc.): the course will be dropped.

Violation 8: a student registers a course with a co-requisite violation: the course will be dropped.

## Violation 9: courses outside the degree plan (out-of-major courses)

An undergraduate student registers courses outside his degree plan (out-of-major courses): all out-of-major courses (except one lowest level course) will be dropped.

## Violation 10: Junior with 100-level or Senior with 100/200-level courses left

A Junior student with remaining 100 level course(s), or a Senior student with remaining 100/200 level courses: a registration hold will be placed on the student record and he will not be able to make any changes to his schedule. If the issue is not resolved, all courses will be dropped after the last day of adding courses. Students should complete the 100 level course(s) before they reach Junior standing, whereas 200 level course(s) should be completed before Senior standing is reached.

## Violation 11: Junior or Senior dropping 100/200-level courses

A Junior student drops 100 level course(s), or a Senior student drops 100/200 level courses, during the registration period: all courses will be dropped after the last day for adding courses. A student should not drop 100/200 level courses as it is against university regulations.

## Violations 12 and 13: graduate and undergraduate course levels

Violation 12: graduate students taking lower than 300 level courses: the lower than 300 level course(s) will be dropped after the last day for adding courses.

Violation 13: undergraduate students taking graduate courses without the required approval: the graduate course will be dropped. Students should contact their department in this regard.

## Violation 14: more than 15 regular terms without graduating

Undergraduate students who have completed more than 15 regular terms and have still not graduated: the student will continue in the course unless otherwise instructed by the Dean, Admissions & Registration.

## Violation 15: Co-op or Internship registration without the required approvals

Students registering for the Co-op or Internship Program with one or more of the following violations, without the required approvals: (1) registering Co-op in the last term before graduation; (2) not having completed 86 credit hours of the degree plan; (3) not fulfilling the prerequisite requirements; (4) having either major or cumulative GPA less than 2.00. Consequence: the registration of the Co-op or Internship Program will be cancelled.

## Violation 16: Summer Training registration without the required approvals

Students registering for the Summer Training program with one or more of the following violations, without the required approvals: (1) registering Summer Training in the last term before graduation; (2) not having completed 66 credit hours of the degree plan; (3) not fulfilling the prerequisite requirements; (4) having either major or cumulative GPA less than 2.00. Consequence: the registration of the Summer Training program will be cancelled.

## Violation 17: no advisor approval before early registration

A student who does not get the advisor approval for early registration before the early registration period will not be able to register during the early registration period.
