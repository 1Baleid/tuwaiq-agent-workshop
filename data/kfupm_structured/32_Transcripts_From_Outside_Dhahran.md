# How to Request Transcripts from Outside Dhahran

## Transcript fee (SAR 11.50) and how to pay it

Each transcript costs SAR 11.50 (including VAT). Deposit the amount in Riyad Bank account No. 3120158799901, IBAN: SA8920000003120158799901. Payment should be in the name of KFUPM.

## How to send the transcript request and what it must contain

Send the copy of the payment receipt along with your request to the office of the University Registrar by fax No. +966138602028 or through email registrar@kfupm.edu.sa. The request for a transcript should contain the student's name and ID number, mailing address, a copy of the Saudi ID card (for Saudis) and a copy of the passport (for non-Saudis).

## How the transcript is delivered (mail, representative or PDF)

The transcript can be mailed to the given address, collected by an authorized representative upon providing appropriate authorization, or issued electronically as a PDF file.
