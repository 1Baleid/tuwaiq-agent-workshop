# Preparatory Students Eligible for Taking Freshmen Courses

## New preparatory students: freshmen courses up to 13 credit hours in total

All new preparatory year students will be given preparatory courses by the Office of the University Registrar. If the total credit hours are < 11, then the students can apply to register freshmen courses such that the total registered hours for both preparatory and freshmen courses should be ≤ 13 credit hours.

A student can register MATH101, MATH105 or MATH106 only after completion of the preparatory MATH002 course.

## Continuing preparatory students with cumulative GPA of 3 or more: up to 14.5 credit hours

All continuing preparatory year students will be given preparatory courses by the Office of the University Registrar. A student with cumulative GPA ≥ 3: if the total credit hours are < 10.5, then the student can register freshmen courses through the KFUPM Portal, such that the total registered hours for both preparatory and freshmen courses should be ≤ 14.5 credit hours. Only one course can be taken from the IAS department.

## Continuing preparatory students with cumulative GPA below 3: up to 10.5 credit hours

All continuing preparatory year students will be given preparatory courses by the Office of the University Registrar. A student with cumulative GPA < 3: if the total credit hours are < 8, then the student can register freshmen courses through the KFUPM Portal such that the total registered hours for both preparatory and freshmen courses should be ≤ 10.5 credit hours. Only one course can be taken from the IAS department.

## Freshmen courses a preparatory student can register

ACCT110, ARC114, ARC122, ARE 100, CE 101, CHEM101, CHEM102, ECON101, ECON102, ENGL101, ENGL102, GEOL102, GEOL103, GEOP102, IAS111, IAS121, IAS131, ICS104, ICS108, ITD101, MATH101, MATH102, MATH105, MATH106, MIS101, PETE101, PHYS101, PHYS102.

The MATH courses (MATH101, MATH102, MATH105, MATH106) need the preparatory MATH002 course completed first.
