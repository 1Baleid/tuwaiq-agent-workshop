How to Request Transcripts from Outside Dhahran

Each transcript costs SAR 11.50 (including VAT). Deposit the amount in Riyad Bank account No.

3120158799901, IBAN: SA8920000003120158799901. Payment should be in the name of KFUPM. Send the copy

of the payment receipt along with your request to the office of the University Registrar by fax No.

+966138602028 or through email registrar@kfupm.edu.sa. The request for a transcript should contain

the student's name and ID number, mailing address, a copy of the Saudi ID card (for Saudis) and a

copy of the passport (for non-Saudis). The transcript can be mailed to the given address, collected

by an authorized representative upon providing appropriate authorization, or issued electronically

as a PDF file.
