All new preparatory year students will be given preparatory courses by the Office of the University
Registrar. If the total credit hours are < 11, then the students can apply to register freshmen
courses such that the total registered hours for both preparatory and freshmen courses should be ≤
13 credit hours. A student can register MATH101, MATH105 or MATH106 only after completion of the
preparatory MATH002 course. All continuing preparatory year students will be given preparatory
courses by the Office of the University Registrar. A student with cumulative GPA ≥ 3: if the total
credit hours are < 10.5, then the student can register freshmen courses through the KFUPM Portal,
such that the total registered hours for both preparatory and freshmen courses should be ≤ 14.5
credit hours. Only one course can be taken from the IAS department. All continuing preparatory year
students will be given preparatory courses by the Office of the University Registrar. A student with
cumulative GPA < 3: if the total credit hours are < 8, then the student can register freshmen
courses through the KFUPM Portal such that the total registered hours for both preparatory and
freshmen courses should be ≤ 10.5 credit hours. Only one course can be taken from the IAS
department. ACCT110, ARC114, ARC122, ARE 100, CE 101, CHEM101, CHEM102, ECON101, ECON102, ENGL101,
ENGL102, GEOL102, GEOL103, GEOP102, IAS111, IAS121, IAS131, ICS104, ICS108, ITD101, MATH101,
MATH102, MATH105, MATH106, MIS101, PETE101, PHYS101, PHYS102. The MATH courses (MATH101, MATH102,
MATH105, MATH106) need the preparatory MATH002 course completed first.
