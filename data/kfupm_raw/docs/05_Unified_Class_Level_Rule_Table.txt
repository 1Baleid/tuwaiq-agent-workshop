Class level rules are unified for the undergraduate level across all majors: Freshman (FR): 0 - 30
credit hours Sophomore (SO): 31 - 62 credit hours Junior (JR): 63 - 96 credit hours Senior (SR): 97
credit hours onwards For early registration, the class level counts the credit hours already earned
plus the credit hours registered in the current term. If a FRESHMAN student is registered in 14
credit hours in term 211, and has already earned 18 credit hours, then the student will be SOPHOMORE
(SO) in term 212 for early registration purposes (14 + 18 = 32 credit hours). If a JUNIOR student is
registered for 16 credit hours in term 211, and has already earned 81 credit hours, then the student
will be SENIOR (SR) in term 212 for early registration purposes (16 + 81 = 97 credit hours). The
steps to check your class level through the portal are at
https://registrar.kfupm.edu.sa/docs/pdf/Steps_to_check_Class_Level.pdf
