Undergraduate early registration for terms 262 and 263 by class level (as of term 262): Senior only,
Saturday 7th Nov 2026: 8 AM - 8:59 AM (term 262 only) and 9 AM - 9:30 AM (terms 262 & 263). Junior
only, Saturday 7th Nov 2026: 9:45 AM - 10:44 AM (term 262 only) and 10:45 AM - 11:15 AM (terms 262 &
263). Sophomore only, Saturday 7th Nov 2026: 11:30 AM - 12:29 PM (term 262 only) and 12:30 PM - 1 PM
(terms 262 & 263). Freshmen only, Saturday 7th Nov 2026: 1:15 PM - 2:14 PM (term 262 only) and 2:15
PM - 2:45 PM (terms 262 & 263). Eligible Preparatory students (expected to be promoted to Freshmen),
Saturday 7th Nov 2026: 3 PM - 3:59 PM (term 262 only) and 4 PM - 4:30 PM (terms 262 & 263). All
undergraduate students and eligible Preparatory students (expected to be promoted to Freshmen) can
register from Saturday 7th Nov 2026 (5 PM onwards) until Sunday 15th Nov 2026 (up to 3 PM), at all
times, for terms 262 and 263. All graduate students can register from Sunday 8th Nov 2026 (4 PM
onwards) until Sunday 15th Nov 2026 (up to 12 noon), at all times, for terms 262 and 263.
Undergraduate students should verify their class level for term 262 through the portal (Registrar
Services tab, Registration Status link) to check their designated registration timings. The minimum
load for undergraduate students in a regular term is 13 credits. Undergraduate students with an
Advisor Approval Hold will not be able to register early for terms 262 and 263 until their academic
plan is locked and the hold is removed by their advisors. Undergraduate students with an SEP Hold
may register only when early registration is open for all undergraduate students, provided their
academic plan is locked and the Advisor Approval Hold has been removed by their advisors.
