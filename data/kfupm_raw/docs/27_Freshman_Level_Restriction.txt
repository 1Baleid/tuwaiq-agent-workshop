Undergraduate students shall be limited to a maximum of three regular terms to complete the Freshman
level, including any dropped, withdrawn, or no-show term. Limiting completion to three terms will
ensure timely progression, promote academic continuity, and support graduation within the prescribed
timeframe. Undergraduate students who do not complete the Freshmen level within three regular terms,
including dropped, withdrawn, or no-show terms, shall be subject to dismissal at the end of the
third regular term.
