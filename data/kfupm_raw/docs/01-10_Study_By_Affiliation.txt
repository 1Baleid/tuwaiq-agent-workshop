Based upon the recommendation of the colleges, the University Council may adopt the principle of
admission by affiliation in some colleges and majors which allow this option. The University Council
sets the rules and regulations for affiliation according to the following parameters: The credit-
hours required for the graduation of an associate student should not be less than the credit-hours
required of a regular student. The associate student will be treated, with regard to admission,
grading, transfer, dismissal and re-enrollment, in exactly the same manner as a regular student
except the requirement regarding class attendance. On the basis of the college council's
recommendation, the University Council determines the rules required to evaluate the performance of
associate students. The student transcript, graduation certificate, and degree, must indicate that
the student has studied "by affiliation".
