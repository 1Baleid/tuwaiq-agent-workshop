Renewals

You can renew a book online up to two times, unless another student has reserved it.

Late returns and lost books

The fine for a late book is 2 SAR per book for each day it is late, up to 60 SAR per book. If you lose a book, you pay the price of a new copy plus a 50 SAR processing fee.

Opening hours

Sunday to Thursday: 8:00 to 22:00. Saturday: 10:00 to 18:00. The library is closed on Fridays.

Quiet floors

Floors 3 and 4 are silent study areas. Group study rooms on floor 2 can be booked for up to 3 hours.
